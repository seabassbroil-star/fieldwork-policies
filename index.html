FIELDWORK / PUBLIC POLICIES

Clear rules for a human-reviewed research tool.

Fieldwork is a local prospect-research assistant. These pages explain the rules for using it and how it handles website research, operator-entered evidence, and transient Google Places data.

Why these pages exist

They provide the public policy disclosures required for the Fieldwork pilot and its use of Google Maps Platform services. They do not claim that Google ratings prove service quality, and they do not replace independent legal review.
