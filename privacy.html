FIELDWORK / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 12, 2026

Scope

Fieldwork runs locally and is intended for research about public businesses, venues, events, and visitor information. It processes selected public source content, operator-entered research, workflow records, patch previews, and authentication sessions.

Google Places data

Fieldwork sends controlled local-search queries to Google Places. Depending on the workflow, live results can include a Place ID, business or venue name, address, status, Google Maps link, website listing, category, rating, rating count, and phone. These fields are displayed as transient discovery or identity signals. Fieldwork durably stores a selected Place ID, which Google permits for later retrieval, but does not store raw Places responses or restricted live field snapshots by default. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Locally stored information

The business workflow can store operator-authored notes, checklists, pipeline state, independently sourced contacts, interactions, follow-up dates, evidence, owned websites, approved crawl findings, and audit history. The editorial workflow can store content type and slug, source URLs and titles, source roles, observation dates, SHA-256 content hashes, short evidence locators, fact decisions, human-reviewed copy, image provenance, verification deadlines, patch previews, and audit history. These are operator research records, not saved Google Places snapshots. Fieldwork never automatically copies a transient Google address, phone, website, rating, or review count into durable research. Session tokens are hashed. API keys remain server-side and must not be placed in this policy repository.

Public source processing

The Research Desk can fetch bounded public HTML, XML, RSS, Atom, or calendar content from operator-approved URLs. It discards the fetched body after extracting limited candidate fields or recording a title, checked date, content hash, and operator-authored evidence locator. It does not store full source pages by default and does not execute source-provided instructions.

Google refresh history

Fieldwork does not currently store Google-derived rating-threshold, website-listed, or business-status history. Place IDs are retained as provider references. Any future derived change tracking is a release-gated feature that requires a documented review against the then-current Google Maps Platform and Places terms before it is enabled.

Retention and deletion

Records remain in the operator-controlled local database until the operator deletes them under an established retention process. Place IDs may be refreshed because provider identifiers can change. Source evidence becomes visibly due after the applicable 30- or 90-day review period but is retained as audit history until deleted. Database backups, exports, and generated patches are controlled by the operator. Public policy repository issues are retained according to GitHub's policies and repository administration.

Sharing

Fieldwork sends search queries or Place-ID requests to Google Places and makes bounded requests to operator-selected public websites and feeds. Its workflow features are local recordkeeping only: it does not automatically send outreach, external notifications, repository pushes, deployments, or public content.

Your controls

The local operator controls candidate selection, source access, fact decisions, copy and patch approval, export, retention, and deletion. Questions or deletion requests concerning a particular installation must be directed to that installation's operator.

Changes

This policy may change as the pilot evolves. The effective date above identifies the current published version.

Contact

Use the public policy issue tracker. Never include credentials, personal customer data, or confidential research in a public issue.
