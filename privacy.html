FIELDWORK / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: July 30, 2026

Scope

Fieldwork runs locally and is intended for public company research. It processes public website content, operator-entered research, workflow records, and authentication sessions.

Google Places data

Fieldwork sends controlled business-search queries to Google Places. Live results can include a Place ID, business name, address, status, Google Maps link, website listing, rating, rating count, and phone. These fields are displayed as transient discovery signals. Fieldwork durably stores the Place ID, which Google permits for later retrieval, but does not store raw Places responses or restricted live field snapshots by default. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Locally stored information

The local database stores operator-authored notes, evidence URLs and labels, observation dates, verification statuses, owned websites, audit history, crawl drafts, assessments, approved company facts, and workflow timestamps. Session tokens are hashed. API keys remain server-side and must not be placed in this policy repository.

Retention and deletion

Records remain in the operator-controlled local database until the operator deletes them under an established retention process. Place IDs may be refreshed because provider identifiers can change. Database backups and exports are controlled by the operator. Public policy repository issues are retained according to GitHub's policies and repository administration.

Sharing

Fieldwork sends search queries and Place-ID refresh requests to Google Places and makes bounded requests to researched public websites. It does not automatically send outreach or publish company records.

Your controls

The local operator controls prospect verification, approval, export, retention, and deletion. Questions or deletion requests concerning a particular installation must be directed to that installation's operator.

Changes

This policy may change as the pilot evolves. The effective date above identifies the current published version.

Contact

Use the public policy issue tracker. Never include credentials, personal customer data, or confidential research in a public issue.
