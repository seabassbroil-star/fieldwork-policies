FIELDWORK / TERMS

Terms of Use

Effective and last updated: July 30, 2026

Purpose

Fieldwork is a local research assistant for human-reviewed business prospect research. It identifies candidates, collects operator-authored evidence, and prepares website research drafts. It does not perform automatic outreach.

Human review

Discovery results, ratings, assessments, and website observations are research signals, not verified statements about service quality. An operator must independently review and approve company facts and assessment dispositions before they are saved as approved company records.

Google Maps Platform

Fieldwork uses Google Places to locate candidate businesses and display current listing and reputation signals. Use of Google Maps content is subject to the Google Maps Platform Terms of Service. Google data may be incomplete or change over time.

Acceptable use

Users must comply with applicable law, provider terms, website terms, and reasonable crawl limits. Users must not use crawled or provider-supplied content as instructions, evade access controls, or treat Fieldwork output as a substitute for professional or legal advice.

No warranty

Fieldwork is provided as-is for a limited research pilot. Accuracy, availability, completeness, and fitness for a particular purpose are not guaranteed.

Changes

These terms may change as the pilot evolves. The effective date above identifies the current published version.
