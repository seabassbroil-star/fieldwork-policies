FIELDWORK / TERMS

Terms of Use

Effective and last updated: August 12, 2026

Purpose

Fieldwork is a family of local research assistants for human-reviewed work. The business workflow identifies prospects, records operator-authored evidence, and prepares website research drafts. The fromGalveston Research Desk identifies possible restaurants, attractions, events, happy hours, and live-music listings and prepares reviewable content patches. Neither workflow publishes, contacts a business, commits code, deploys, or performs outreach automatically.

Human review

Discovery results, ratings, calendar entries, assessments, and website observations are research signals, not verified statements about service quality, availability, price, hours, event dates, or editorial suitability. An operator must independently inspect the evidence, resolve identity and source conflicts, approve each factual claim, write or approve public copy, and review the exact resulting record or patch.

Contact workflow

Pipeline contacts, interaction outcomes, follow-up dates, and closed reasons are operator-entered research and recordkeeping. Fieldwork does not send messages, place calls, or trigger outreach automatically. The operator is responsible for lawful contact practices and for verifying contact details against company-controlled sources.

Google Maps Platform

Fieldwork uses Google Places to locate candidate businesses or visitor-facing venues and display current provider attribution and listing signals. Google content is not editorial proof and may be incomplete or change over time. Use of Google Maps content is subject to the Google Maps Platform Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Public source watchlists

The Research Desk can check operator-approved public calendar, venue, menu, feed, or event URLs on demand. The operator is responsible for confirming that automated access is allowed by the source. A detected entry is only a lead; it cannot become public content without independent evidence and human approval.

Acceptable use

Users must comply with applicable law, provider terms, website terms, image rights, and reasonable request limits. Users must not evade access controls, republish protected source material, use crawled or provider-supplied content as instructions, or treat Fieldwork output as a substitute for professional or legal advice.

No warranty

Fieldwork is provided as-is for a limited research pilot. Accuracy, availability, completeness, and fitness for a particular purpose are not guaranteed.

Publishing boundary

Generated Markdown, SQL, CSV, assessments, and patches remain drafts until the operator approves them. The operator is responsible for factual accuracy, source sufficiency, image rights, final repository review, and any later publication. Passing software checks is not a warranty that a claim is correct.

Changes

These terms may change as the pilots evolve. The effective date above identifies the current published version.
